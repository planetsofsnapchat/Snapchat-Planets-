Snapchat Planets Order: All 8 Planets and What They Mean

If you opened your friend's profile and saw a planet, you are looking at the Snapchat Friend Solar System. This visual feature ranks your best friends and shows each one as a planet. You are the Sun in the middle.

1. Mercury - Your closest friend. The friend you snap and chat with the most shows up here.
2. Venus - Your second closest friend, right behind Mercury.
3. Earth - Third position. Still one of your most active friendships.
4. Mars - Fourth. A regular friend you talk to often.
5. Jupiter - Fifth. Someone you interact with, but less than the top four.
6. Saturn - Sixth. Occasional snaps and chats.
7. Uranus - Seventh. A friend you barely interact with.
8. Neptune - Eighth and farthest. The friend you talk to the least among your top eight.

How does Snapchat decide the order?

The order comes from your Best Friends ranking. Snapchat looks at who you snap and chat with most often over recent weeks, and the top eight friends get planet positions. When your activity changes, the order changes too. The full how Snapchat best friends ranking works guide explains which actions count the most.

Why did my planet change or disappear?

A planet changes when someone's ranking moves up or down. Planets can disappear if the Friend Solar System feature is turned off in Snapchat+ settings, or after a Snapchat update resets settings. The planets only appear for Snapchat+ subscribers on both sides of the friendship.

Do I need Snapchat+ to see planets?

Yes. Friend Solar Systems are part of Snapchat+, the paid subscription. Both you and the friend need Snapchat+ for the planet to show on that friend's profile.

FAQ

Which planet means best friend on Snapchat?

Mercury. It is the first planet and means the friend you interact with the most.

What does Neptune mean on Snapchat?

Neptune is the eighth planet, the friend you interact with the least among your top eight.

Can two friends have the same planet?

No. Each of your top eight friends gets a different planet, from Mercury to Neptune.

Why can't I see planets?

You need Snapchat+ on both sides, the feature turned on in settings, and a recent app version.
